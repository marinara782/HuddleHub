      How it works

      Connection and promotion, working together

      Getting into adaptive sport is often hard because programs are scattered and people don't know who else plays. HuddleHub fixes both sides.

      1. 01

        Make a profile

        Pick your sports, your role (athlete, caregiver, coach, volunteer) and any support you need.

      2. 02

        Meet your people

        Find teammates, training partners, mentors and other families who play the same sports.

      3. 03

        Find a hub

        See which adaptive hubs offer your sport, plus equipment loans, funding help or rides.

      4. 04

        Hubs reach more people

        Hubs are shown to the members most likely to join, so programs fill up.

      For hub organizers

      Get your hub in front of the people looking for it

      Nonprofit and community hubs list free. Sponsors and businesses can pay for extra visibility, which keeps HuddleHub free for members.
